# Consent And Safety Copy

Status: alpha launch copy contract.

Legal, consent, billing, and safety copy must stay plain, static, and readable. It must not rely on the Lina Midnight Channel UI style to soften warnings.

## Explicit 18+ Gate

Title:

`18+ explicit access`

Body:

`This area may include explicit sexual or adult AI content. By continuing, you confirm that you are at least 18 years old and that you will not request illegal, non-consensual, exploitative, minor-related, abusive, or harmful content.`

Primary action:

`I am 18+ and accept`

Secondary action:

`Keep locked`

Backend result copy:

- Accepted: `Explicit access is enabled for this account.`
- Rejected or unavailable: `Explicit access remains locked.`
- Backend failure: `Could not update explicit access. Try again later.`

## Restricted Explicit Persona

Locked state:

`Locked: 18+ consent required.`

Locked detail:

`This channel requires explicit access. Confirm 18+ consent before using it.`

Blocked by tier:

`Locked: upgrade or trial access required.`

Blocked by policy:

`Unavailable under current safety policy.`

## Abuse And Reporting Copy

Bot support prompt:

`To report abuse, payment trouble, account removal, or privacy requests, contact the alpha operator channel and include your Telegram username plus a short description. Do not include passwords, payment card numbers, or private documents.`

Mini App profile/support copy:

`Need help or want to report a problem? Contact alpha support with your Telegram username and a short description.`

Abuse acknowledgement:

`Report received. An operator will review it. Access may be limited while the review is open.`

## Deletion And Export Copy

Export request acknowledgement:

`Export request received. Alpha support will prepare available account, usage, consent, and conversation/event records.`

Deletion request acknowledgement:

`Deletion request received. Alpha support will confirm identity, reset removable conversation state, revoke explicit consent on request, and minimize retained records where operationally possible. Payment, entitlement, fraud, abuse, and operator audit records may be retained when needed for disputes, accounting, or safety.`

Consent revocation:

`Explicit access has been locked again for this account.`

## Operator Copy Constraints

Operators must not promise:

- guaranteed deletion of payment, entitlement, abuse, or audit records;
- legal compliance beyond the alpha policy;
- emergency response;
- medical, legal, financial, psychological, or safety-critical advice;
- public-launch availability or unchanged pricing/limits.
