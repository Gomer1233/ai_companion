# Lina Alpha Terms

Status: alpha launch draft. These terms are for the closed Telegram alpha and must be reviewed before public launch, paid expansion, or a standalone web launch.

## Eligibility

Lina alpha is available only to invited users. Users must comply with Telegram terms and applicable law.

Explicit 18+ personas and image capabilities are available only to users who confirm they are at least 18 years old and accept the explicit content gate.

## Service Description

Lina provides AI companion conversations, persona/channel selection, limited image generation, entitlement/usage state, and a Telegram Mini App guide. AI responses are generated by third-party providers and may be inaccurate, incomplete, delayed, or unavailable.

Lina is not a professional service and must not be used as a substitute for medical, legal, financial, psychological, emergency, or safety-critical advice.

## User Responsibilities

Users must not use Lina to:

- create, request, or distribute illegal content;
- request sexual content involving minors or age-ambiguous people;
- request non-consensual, exploitative, coercive, abusive, or harassing sexual content;
- generate instructions for violence, self-harm, fraud, credential theft, malware, or other abuse;
- upload or provide personal data about others without permission;
- attempt to bypass access, payment, rate limit, consent, or moderation controls;
- overload, scrape, reverse engineer, or disrupt the service.

Operators may restrict, suspend, or remove access for policy violations, abuse risk, payment issues, or alpha capacity limits.

## Explicit 18+ Content

Explicit content access requires a separate consent action. Consent confirms that the user:

- is at least 18 years old;
- understands explicit AI content may be sexual, suggestive, or adult-oriented;
- agrees not to request illegal, non-consensual, exploitative, minor-related, or harmful content;
- understands access can be revoked after abuse or policy changes.

The frontend can display the consent path, but backend policy decides whether explicit access is allowed.

## Payments And Entitlements

Alpha may include free, trial, premium, and manually granted access states. Payment providers process payment instruments; Lina stores order and entitlement metadata.

Entitlements can be granted, fulfilled, repaired, revoked, refunded, cancelled, or expire according to backend records and support decisions. Alpha pricing, limits, and availability may change before public launch.

## Availability

Alpha is provided as-is for testing. Railway, Vercel, Telegram, payment providers, AI providers, or database services can cause downtime or degraded behavior. Operators may pause features, providers, personas, payments, or the Mini App during incidents.

## Data And Privacy

Data handling is described in `privacy-policy.md`. Users can request export, deletion/reset, consent revocation, or support through the alpha support process in `ops-runbook.md`.

## Content Ownership And License

Users retain rights they have in their own inputs. By using Lina, users allow Lina and configured providers to process inputs and outputs as needed to provide, secure, debug, and support the alpha.

AI outputs may be similar across users and may not be unique. Users are responsible for how they use outputs.

## Termination

Operators may remove a user from the closed alpha, revoke explicit consent, revoke entitlements, or restrict access for abuse, legal risk, platform policy risk, non-payment, capacity limits, or operational need.

These alpha terms can change as the product and provider policies change. Material changes should be communicated through the closed alpha support channel or bot/Mini App copy before expanded launch.
